## Abstract
---
- Group multiple cables in different switch ports into one logical port (port channel interface)

>[!important] More speed and redundancy
> We are combining the [[Bandwidth|bandwidth]] of multiple cables into one, and multiple physical cables give better fault tolerance when some of the cable are broken.


>[!info] EtherChannel
> Same idea, just a different name used by Cisco.
