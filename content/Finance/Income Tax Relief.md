## Abstract
---
- An individual can claim up to **SGD 80,000** in tax relief annually.
- You can check the tax reliefs available to you [here](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-reliefs-rebates-and-deductions/tax-reliefs)

>[!tip] Some tips
> Check out Milelion to find the best ways to pay your income tax.
> 
> Topping up your SRS account provides greater value if you have a higher income, as higher tax rates apply to larger taxable amounts.
> 
> Learn more about the [NSman Tax Relief](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-reliefs-rebates-and-deductions/tax-reliefs/nsman-relief-(self-wife-and-parent)).
> 
> Top up your CPF MediSave Account (MA), as healthcare costs are high and such expenses are very likely to arise.
